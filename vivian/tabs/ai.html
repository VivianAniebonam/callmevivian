AI Development Sprint Process

  1. Discovery & Requirements (2 weeks): Clinical workflow analysis, AI use case definition, data availability assessment, success metrics definition.
  2. Model Development & Training: Coordinate with AI team on training data preparation, model architecture selection, training iterations, and validation testing.
  3. Integration Sprints (2-week cycles): Integrate AI inference endpoints into backend services, develop frontend visualization of AI outputs, implement real-time prediction delivery.
  4. Clinical Pilot Testing: Deploy to pilot sites with close monitoring, collect radiologist feedback, measure impact on turnaround times and diagnostic quality.
  5. Production Deployment: Rollout with CI/CD automation, establish monitoring dashboards for model performance, implement feedback loops for continuous improvement.
